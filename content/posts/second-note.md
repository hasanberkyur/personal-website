I keep a simple rule for product systems: if the workflow feels stressful, it is already
too complex. Calm systems make teams faster because they remove uncertainty.

Here is the checklist I start with:

- Fewer moving parts, not just better dashboards.
- A single source of truth for status.
- Clear ownership and handoffs that do not depend on heroics.

When these basics are solid, iteration speeds up naturally.
